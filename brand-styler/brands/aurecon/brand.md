# Aurecon pack notes

Quirks the tokens can't express:

- **Aurecon Green `89C925` as text**: too light to read as type on white. Green
  text must be Accessible Green `6AA41E`, bold or large (>=18pt), on white or a
  very light background only. On a dark cover, `89C925` reads well and is the
  correct accent there.
- **Never substitute another green** (e.g. sea green `2E8B57`) for Aurecon
  Green - it is the most common off-brand drift.
- **One green moment per slide.** If two things are green, nothing is.
- The tertiary ramp is for charts/infographics only, in its listed order:
  Dark Green, Dark Blue, Yellow, Melon, Teal Blue, Accessible Green.
- Cover/closer: Aurecon Grey `373A36` or Grey 5 `1C1B1C`, white content slides
  between them (the sandwich).
